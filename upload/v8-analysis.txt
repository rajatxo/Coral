Here is the concrete analysis of your **current** ViTune-BC build based on the 16 screenshots provided.

### **The Core Problem**
You are currently running a **"Full-Bleed / Hero Image" layout**. The album art is stretched to fill the entire width of the screen (or close to it), acting as the background for the top half of the player. This is why you cannot see the "blurred background around it"—the image *is* the background.

To get back to the "before" look, you need to switch the **Album Art Mode** from `Full Width` or `Fit` to **`Square`** or **`Fixed Size`**.

### **Summary of What Needs Fixing**

To restore the **"Before" (Standard)** look:

1.  **Change Album Art Constraint:**
    *   **Current:** `match_parent` (width) with `wrap_content` (height) or similar.
    *   **Target:** Fixed size (e.g., `300dp x 300dp`) or Aspect Ratio `1:1`, centered horizontally with margins on the left and right.
2.  **Fix the Background Layer:**
    *   Currently, the background seems to be hidden or the opacity is 0 because the foreground art covers it all.
    *   Once you shrink the art to a square, the **Blurred Background** will automatically become visible in the gaps around the square.
3.  **Fix the Gradient:**
    *   The black gradient currently sits on top of the giant image.
    *   In the "Before" version, this gradient should ideally be part of the background container, fading from transparent (at the top) to dark (at the bottom), sitting *behind* the text but potentially *over* the blurred background.

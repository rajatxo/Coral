### **Overall Synthesis & Design Specs**

#### **1. Overall Visual Style**
*   **Theme:** Deep Dark Mode (OLED black `#121212` or darker).
*   **Aesthetic:** **Glassmorphism / Neo-Morphism**. It uses transparency, blurs (implied by the glossy look), and gradients rather than flat Material Design colors.
*   **Typography:** Sans-serif (likely Roboto or Inter). Titles are Bold/Large, Artists are Medium/Grey.
*   **Vibe:** Modern, sleek, "iOS-meets-Android" hybrid. High contrast text on dark backgrounds.

#### **2. Mini Player / Now Playing Bar**
*   **Position:** Fixed at the bottom, above the system navigation bar.
*   **Shape:** **Capsule/Pill shape** (fully rounded corners).
*   **Effect:** This is the standout feature. It uses a **metallic/glossy gradient** (likely a linear gradient of Teal/Cyan `#2d9f9e` fading to Dark Grey `#333`). It looks like frosted glass.
*   **Components:**
    *   Left: Circular Album Art (small).
    *   Center: Title & Artist (2 lines).
    *   Right: "Device/Output" icon (headphone/speaker) and "Favorite" (Heart outline).
*   **Shadow:** Subtle drop shadow to lift it off the background.

#### **3. Playlist Section (The "Glossy Look")**
*   **Card Shape:** **Super Ellipse / Squircle** (rounded rectangle but very round).
*   **Fill:** Semi-transparent gradient fills.
    *   *Favorites:* Dark Red tint (`#4a1515` approx).
    *   *Offline:* Dark Blue tint (`#152b4a` approx).
*   **Border:** Thin, subtle border matching the gradient color (e.g., red border for favorites).
*   **Content:** Icon + Text centered or left-aligned within the pill.

#### **4. Lyrics Display**
*   **Location:** Overlaid on the lower half of the Album Art area (or replacing the description area).
*   **Behavior:** The background image dims (black overlay with ~60% opacity) to ensure text readability.
*   **Typography:**
    *   Active Line: White, Bold, Larger (~18sp).
    *   Inactive Lines: Grey (`#888`), Regular weight, Smaller (~15sp).
*   **Animation:** Implies smooth scrolling (Karaoke style) where the active line centers itself.

#### **5. Timeline / Progress Bar**
*   **Shape:** Thin line (2-3px height).
*   **Color:** Light Grey or White for the track, Accent Color (Cyan/Blue) for the filled progress.
*   **Thumb:** A circular white handle (approx 12px diameter) that sits on the line.
*   **Position:** Below the metadata/lyrics snippet, above the main playback controls.

#### **6. Bottom Navigation / Tab Bar**
*   **Type:** **Vertical Navigation Rail** (on the left side) OR just text labels if viewing a specific context, but the screenshots show a persistent **Left Navigation Rail**.
*   **Items:** Quick picks, Discover, Songs, Playlists, Artists, Albums, Local.
*   **Style:**
    *   Text is vertical (rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise).
    *   Icons sit above the text (or beside it depending on width, here looks like Icon + Vertical Text stack).
    *   **Active State:** The active tab text is bold/white. Inactive tabs are grey.
    *   **Indicator:** A floating pill shape or highlight appears behind the active item (seen in Screenshot 3).

#### **7. Album Art Treatment**
*   **Shape:** Rounded Rectangle (Square aspect ratio).
*   **Radius:** Approx 20dp-30dp (quite rounded, not fully circular unless in mini-player).
*   **Shadows:** Soft drop shadow to separate from the dark background.
*   **In Player:** Large scale, centered. When lyrics are active, the art gets a dark scrim overlay.

*   **Background:** `#121212` (Deep Black)
*   **Surface/Card BG:** `#1E1E1E`
*   **Primary Accent:** **Teal / Cyan** (`#26A69A` or `#009688`) -> Used for "Queue" button, Progress bar, Active states.

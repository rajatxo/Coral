Here is the critical analysis of your **ViTune-BC** build based on the 18 screenshots provided.

### **Overall Visual Diagnosis**
Your app is suffering from a "Bottom-Heavy" layout syndrome. The controls are pushed too far down, the background blur is likely using the wrong source (or is transparent), and the UI elements are fighting for space at the very bottom edge of the screen (the "danger zone" near the Android navigation bar).

Below is the breakdown for each specific issue you reported, with concrete measurements to fix them.

---

### **1. The Blend Issue (The "Hard Seam")**
*   **What I see:** There is a distinct horizontal line cutting across the screen where the sharp album art (top) meets the darkened/blurred area (bottom).
*   **The Problem:** This looks like a `Modifier.blur()` or a gradient overlay that starts abruptly.
*   **Location:** The seam is located at approximately **55% - 60%** of the screen height (roughly halfway down the artist's nose/cheek area).
*   **Why it looks bad:** In high-quality music apps (like BITCHORD or Apple Music), this transition is usually a very smooth, long gradient (often starting from the top and fading to dark at the bottom), or the blur covers the *entire* image uniformly without a hard cut line.
*   **Fix:** You need to extend the "darkening" gradient to start higher up (around 30% height) so it fades gently, or ensure the blur effect applies to the full canvas before overlaying the scrim.

### **2. Blur Transparency Issue ("Seeing through the screen")**
*   **What I see:** The bottom half of the screen (where the controls are) looks like a flat, dark grey/black void or a very weak blur.
*   **The Problem:** If you can see "through" it, your `Box` or `Surface` containing the blur likely has `alpha < 1f` or the background color is not opaque enough.
*   **Analysis:**
    *   It does **not** look like it's showing a blurred version of the current album art (which is what it should do). It looks like a generic dark overlay.
    *   If you see your phone's wallpaper *behind* the player, your window background is transparent.
*   **Fix:** The blur container must have an opaque background (e.g., `Color.Black.copy(alpha = 0.6f)` behind the blur layer). Increase the **Blur Radius** to at least **20dp - 30dp** to make it look "glassy" rather than "foggy."

### **3. Button Placement (Shuffle/Repeat/Loop/Menu)**
*   **What I see:** These icons are positioned at the extreme bottom of the layout, almost touching the system navigation bar (the line with Back/Home/Recents).
*   **Vertical Position:** They are sitting at roughly **90% - 95%** of the screen height.
*   **Comparison to Reference:** In BITCHORD/Apple Music style, these secondary buttons usually sit comfortably above the main Play/Pause button row, or in a row *above* the very bottom edge.
*   **Risk:** On phones with gesture navigation, placing tappable icons this low causes accidental "Home" or "Back" gestures when the user tries to tap Shuffle or Loop.
*   **Fix:** Move this entire row up by about **40dp - 60dp**. They should be vertically centered between the SeekBar and the bottom of the safe area.

### **4. Play/Pause Button Size**
*   **What I see:** The Play/Pause icon (|| / ▶) is visually small compared to the surrounding text and the "Queue" pill.
*   **Comparison:** In the reference style, the Play button is the "Hero" element—it should be large (e.g., **72dp - 84dp** size) and prominent.
*   **Current State:** It looks like it might be sized around **48dp** or just using default icon size, making it look thin and insignificant.
*   **Fix:** Wrap the Play icon in a larger clickable area or increase the `ContentScale` / `IconSize` significantly.

### **5. Floating Queue Pill (Major Usability Bug)**
*   **What I see:** The grey "Queue" pill is floating directly on top of the control row.
*   **Overlap:** It is physically covering the center-bottom area. When the Queue expands (Screenshot 13, 17, 18), it completely obscures the Shuffle/Repeat buttons and pushes content off-screen.
*   **Tap Target Blocking:** Even when collapsed, the "Queue" pill is dangerously close to the Play button, creating a "fat finger" risk where a user tries to Pause but opens the Queue instead.
*   **Fix:**
    *   **Vertical Shift:** Move the Queue pill down so it sits *below* the Shuffle/Repeat row, or move the Shuffle/Repeat row *up*.
    *   **Anchor:** Ideally, the Queue pill should be anchored to the very bottom (above nav bar), and the Shuffle/Repeat buttons should be **above** the Play button, not below it.

### **6. Background Shrinking on Pause**
*   **What I see:** Comparing Screenshot 1 (Playing) vs Screenshot 10 (Paused/Tapping):
    *   The blurred background seems to shift or the overlay intensity changes slightly, but more importantly, the **UI density** changes.
    *   When paused, the "Queue" pill behavior or the animation state might be triggering a layout re-measurement that causes the background image to appear to "jump" or shrink because the foreground column height changed.
*   **Fix:** Ensure the `Box` holding the background image has a fixed `fillMaxSize()` constraint that does not change when the state changes from Playing to Paused. Do not wrap the background in an `AnimatedVisibility` that scales it.

### **7. Title & Artist Placement**
*   **What I see:**
    *   **Title:** "Fujii Kaze..." is placed around **58%** height.
    *   **Artist:** "Fujii Kaze" is immediately below it.
    *   **Lyrics:** The lyric text ("San domo no meshi...") appears around **65%** height.
*   **Spacing:** The gap between the Artist name and the Lyrics is good, but the gap between the Album Art face and the Title text feels tight because the "Hard Seam" (Issue #1) cuts right through the middle of the composition.
*   **Fix:** Add **16dp - 24dp** padding above the Title to give it breathing room from the image.

### **8. Overall Layout Comparison (vs. BITCHORD Signature)**
*   **Current Layout (Yours):**
    *   Top 55%: Image
    *   55%-60%: Hard Line
    *   60%-75%: Text/Lyrics/Seekbar
    *   75%-85%: Play Controls (Too small)
    *   85%-95%: Secondary Controls (Too low)
    *   90%-100%: Queue Pill (Overlapping)
*   **Target Layout (BITCHORD Style):**
    *   **Background:** Full screen image, heavily blurred at the bottom 60% (smooth gradient).
    *   **Controls:** All controls (Play, Next, Prev, Shuffle) should be grouped in the **bottom 30%** of the screen, but with comfortable padding (**bottomPadding = 40dp+**) to clear the nav bar.
    *   **Hierarchy:** The Play button must be the largest visual element.

### **Summary of Code Fixes Needed**

If you are editing the Compose file (`PlayerScreen.kt` or similar), apply these specific values:

1.  **Fix the Bottom Padding:**
    Add `padding(bottom = 48.dp)` to your main Column. This pushes everything up so it doesn't overlap the Android Nav Bar.
2.  **Fix the Blur:**
    Use `Modifier.blur(25.dp, RenderEffect)` on the AsyncImage, and put a `Brush.verticalGradient(colors = listOf(Color.Transparent, Color.Black.copy(alpha = 0.8f)))` over the bottom half.
3.  **Fix Button Sizes:**
    Set Play Button size to **72.dp**. Set Shuffle/Repeat icons to **32.dp**.
4.  **Fix Queue Pill:**
    Move it out of the main control flow. Place it at the absolute bottom of the screen (anchored to `Alignment.BottomCenter`) and let the control buttons float **above** it.

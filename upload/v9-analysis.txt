### **3. CONCRETE FIX PLAN (XML/Layout Values)**

To fix this, you need to **reduce the size of the Album Art** (or its top margin) to pull all controls up, and explicitly add padding to the bottom parent container.

#### **A. Adjust Album Art Size**
Your current album art is likely `match_parent` with a fixed aspect ratio (e.g., 1:1) which pushes everything else down on tall screens.
*   **Change:** Constrain the height or add a bottom margin.
*   **Target Value:** Set the ImageView height to roughly **`280dp` - `320dp`** (instead of filling 50% of the screen) OR set `layout_marginBottom="40dp"` on the Album Art view to force the text up.

#### **B. Fix the Bottom Container (The Critical Fix)**
You need to ensure the parent layout (likely a ConstraintLayout or LinearLayout wrapping the bottom section) has padding so content doesn't touch the edge.

**Add this to your Root Layout or Bottom Container:**
```xml
android:paddingBottom="48dp" 
android:clipToPadding="false"
```
*(Note: 48dp accounts for the standard Android nav bar height + some safety margin).*

#### **C. Specific Positioning (ConstraintLayout Example)**
If you are using `ConstraintLayout`, update the constraints for the bottom elements:

1.  **Secondary Controls Row (Shuffle/Repeat/Menu):**
    *   Constrain Bottom to **Top of Chevron**.
    *   Add Margin: `android:layout_marginBottom="24dp"` (Creates space between buttons and the arrow).

2.  **Chevron Up Arrow:**
    *   Constrain Bottom to **Parent Bottom**.
    *   Add Margin: `android:layout_marginBottom="16dp"` (Keeps it above the Nav Bar).

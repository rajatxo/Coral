### **Screenshot 1 & 2 (Song: "Miles Away")**
**Description:** The player is displaying the song "Miles Away". The album art (flowers/sun) fills the entire screen background. The text and controls are overlaid on top of this image.

*   **1. Album Art Size:** **CRITICAL FAILURE.** The album art is being used as a full-screen background (approx. **100% width x 100% height**). There is no distinct, centered square album art image.
    *   *Fix:* You must introduce a dedicated `ImageView` for the album art that is roughly **55-60% of the screen width**, centered horizontally in the upper half of the screen (margin top ~10-15%). The current full-screen stretch looks like a wallpaper, not a music player.
*   **2. Background Visibility:** N/A (Static state).
*   **3. Queue Button:** A large, floating "Queue" pill button is visible at the bottom center (approx. **56dp height**). It has a blue background color sampled from the artwork. It sits awkwardly between the playback controls and the navigation bar.
*   **4. Layout Issues:** The text ("Miles Away") is hard to read because it's overlaid directly on the busy background image without a sufficient blur or scrim (dark overlay). The layout lacks structure; it looks like a slideshow, not an app interface.

### **Screenshot 3 (Transition / List View)**
**Description:** The user has tapped "Next" or selected a new song. We see the list view, but the player area at the bottom shows a transition state or a loading state for the next track.

*   **1. Album Art Size:** In the mini-player bar at the bottom, the thumbnail is appropriately small. However, looking at the main content area (the list), there is no large art display here, which is correct for a list view.
*   **2. Background Visibility:** **CRITICAL FAILURE.** Observe the bottom player bar and the background behind the list. When the song changed from the colorful "Miles Away" to the sepia-toned "Miftah Zaman", the background likely flashed or instantly swapped. If the main player view was open during this switch, the user would see a jarring cut from one full-screen image to another.
    *   *Fix:* Implement a **cross-fade animation (300-400ms)** on the background/album art. Never swap backgrounds instantaneously.
*   **3. Queue Button:** The "Queue" pill is present at the bottom. It has changed color to orange/brown, indicating it's dynamically sampling colors from the *new* song's art, which causes a jarring color flash during transitions.
*   **4. Layout Issues:** The list view looks standard, but the "Now Playing" bar at the bottom feels disconnected because the big "Queue" button below it doesn't belong to the list UI; it belongs to the (hidden) Player UI, creating visual clutter.

### **Summary of Required Fixes**

1.  **Resize Album Art:** Stop using art as background. Create a central `ImageView` (size: **~220dp-280dp** depending on screen density, roughly **55-60% of width**). Position it in the top-center.
2.  **Fix Backgrounds:** Use a **blurred version** (radius ~20dp) and **darkened** (alpha 0.6-0.7) version of the album art as the background. This ensures text is always readable and transitions are smooth.
3.  **Animate Transitions:** Add a **300ms Crossfade** when changing the background image and album art.
4.  **Restyle Queue Button:**
    *   Move it out of the main flow or integrate it into the control row (shuffle/left, repeat/right).
    *   If it must be a floating button, make it smaller (**40dp circle**) and position it above the playback controls, not below them.
    *   **Stop dynamic coloring** it with the album art dominant color (or make the color change much more subtle). The rainbow effect (Blue->Orange->Purple->Pink) is chaotic. Use a neutral surface color (e.g., `#E0E0E0` Dark Grey or `#FFFFFF` White with transparency).
